About UrbanGlow Salon

UrbanGlow Salon is a beauty and grooming company founded in Pretoria in 2022.

The salon provides beauty services, skincare procedures and hair styling.

Our aim is to become the best salon in the area, recognised for excellence and customer satisfaction.

We provide our customers with high-quality beauty services in a calm and professional environment.
